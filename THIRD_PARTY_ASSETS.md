## ShareAlike

The Z-Anatomy-derived GLB and manifest must be distributed under CC BY-SA
with the attributions above. The rest of this project's code is unaffected
(the model is a separate asset).

## Excluded on purpose

Z-Anatomy bundles some models under **non-commercial** licences — "Anatomy of
the Inner Ear" (University of Dundee, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and "Kidney"
(lissiecowley, CC BY-NC 4.0). They are **not** part of the exported subset;
keep them out if the scope grows (see `scripts/anatomy/z-anatomy/README.md`).
